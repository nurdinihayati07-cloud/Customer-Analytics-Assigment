☕ Project Summary: CAFLO Smart Mug
The project centers on CaffiTrack Sdn. Bhd., a Malaysian company founded in 2024. The core mission is to inspire healthier choices by making everyday habits smarter through technology, with a vision to become a leading brand in smart wellness products across South Asia. The motivation behind the brand is the belief that caffeine should help users focus, not disrupt their balance, empowering people to "sip smart, stay aware and thrive".

📱 Product Line
The presentation introduces a focused product ecosystem aimed at health tracking:

1. CAFLO One (Flagship Product): A Smart Mug that syncs with the mobile app to monitor caffeine consumed and allows users to customize the drink temperature and set personal caffeine limits based on health goals.
2. CAFLO Core (Mobile App): The companion app for iOS/Android that provides caffeine intake tracking, real-time temperature display, and synchronization with smartwatches.
3. CAFLO Mini: A compact, travel-ready tumbler designed to keep drinks warm while tracking caffeine intake via the CAFLO app, ideal for travelers, students, and busy coffee lovers.
4. CAFLO Kids: A colorful mug that tracks hydration (water intake) instead of caffeine to encourage healthy habits, which comes with stickers and a parent control app.
